Built for the couch

Every feature designed for your TV — and tuned for your phone.

🎮 Player

ExoPlayer engine with swipe seek, brightness and volume gestures, double-tap to skip, OP/ED auto-skip, PiP, resize modes and playback up to 50x.

📺 Remote-first UI

Full D-pad navigation with visible focus borders on every element, auto-focus on first item and logical focus chains — no mouse required.

📊 Tracking

AniList and MyAnimeList OAuth with auto-updating progress, Rescue Mode when a server is down, and list status notifications.

🔌 Community extensions

Tachiyomi-compatible extension system: add repositories, install from 100+ community-maintained anime sources with language and NSFW filters.

🎨 Themes & OLED

9 accent colors, swap-color pairs, light / dark / OLED variants, frosted glass effect and pixel-tunable NavPill.

📝 Subtitles

Full styling control — font, color, outline, background, size, alpha — with online subs from Wyzie & Stremio and tap-a-cue sync.

🔔 Notifications

Airing anime alerts, show subscriptions, AniList replies and activity mentions, with configurable check frequencies.

🛠️ Backup & Logs

Backup and restore in plain .ani or encrypted .sani, cache cleaner, and a live 50K-line logcat viewer.

Two steps — Install

Install the app, add your extensions, and you're watching.

Step 01

Download

Tap Download APK and pick arm64-v8a — it fits nearly every TV and phone from the last few years. Once it downloads, tap the file to install. An .apk is how Android apps install outside the Play Store.

Step 02

Add extensions

Open Sanin and go to Settings → Extensions. Tap + and paste the repository URL below — Sanin fetches the extension list and every source installs in seconds.

Troubleshooting — Quick fixes

“App not installed”

Usually an old copy is in the way, or the download was incomplete. Uninstall any old Sanin, re-download fully, and pick arm64-v8a (or the universal APK if unsure).

A title won't play

Tap the source name at the top and switch, or pick a different episode server. If a whole source looks dead, install another extension.

Sources won't load

Check your internet and try again. Some ISPs throttle streaming — try a different DNS (Settings → Common → DNS) or a VPN.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one. Sign in to AniList or MyAnimeList only to sync your list and history across devices.
